Eight escape-time sets, plus the ones you save. Pick one, then pan and zoom until something looks worth keeping. Drag a card by its grip to reorder it, or into a folder.

    Ported from a PenguinMod project. Renders on the GPU. WASD pan · Q E zoom · Space saves a PNG

    Fractal

    One formula, iterated until it escapes, from starting values you set. Add variables of your own. Render it once, or save it to the menu.

    z c x y numbers i pi e + − × ÷ ^ ( ) and | | nest in colour abs re im conj exp log sqrt sin cos tan sinh cosh tanh |z| is abs z

    x y the real and imaginary parts of the point being checked, in a starting value or the formula z₀ = 0 with c = x+yi is the Mandelbrot set an added variable takes x and y as well, and holds its value as c does

    Save adds it to the menu under this name, and keeps it in this browser.

    Fixed in this first version: the orbit escapes at |z| > 100, and the zoom stops at 10⁶.
